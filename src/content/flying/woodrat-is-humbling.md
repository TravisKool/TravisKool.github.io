Two weeks at Woodrat before the Rat Route, flying with locals, with no
obligation other than to be there for whatever the days did.

## Conditions

A couple of the days were post-frontal, which at Woodrat means beautiful clouds
and a lot of energy in the sky. Those are the days you show up for and the days
that will quietly hand you your limits.

## The flight

I came in with ambitious goals. I set new personal records for the site, which
is not nothing. I did not achieve the larger goals I had written down before I
arrived.

Both of those things are true at once and it took me a few days to stop treating
that as a contradiction. Woodrat is a humbling place to fly. It is the site I
have flown more than any other, and it still has more to say to me than I have
been able to hear.

## Decisions I would change

The honest answer is that the goals were the right size and I was not ready for
them yet — not that they were wrong. Which is the reason to go back rather than
a reason to write them down smaller.

Next season.
