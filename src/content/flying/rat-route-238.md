The annual Rat Route 238 Fly-In, which pulls pilots from across the US — up to
120 of them — to Woodrat for a week.

## The event

The organizers do a terrific job, and it is worth being specific about why. The
flying is the point, usually twice a day, but the week is built around
everything that makes a fly-in more than a queue for launch: live music, food
trucks, games, wineries, knowledge talks, and weather discussions that are
genuinely useful rather than ceremonial.

That last part is underrated. A week of standing around with 120 pilots talking
about what the day is going to do, then going and finding out, is a faster
feedback loop than any amount of solo flying.

## The wing

I demoed a Gin GTO 3 in size L. It runs a 6.5 aspect ratio against the 6.1 on
the Nova Vortex I had been flying — both two-liners, so it is a step up rather
than a change of category, but it is a real step.

It was immediately obvious that I was not going to want to hand it back, so I
bought it before the week was out. It went straight to Chelan for the Ozone
competitions, which is not the most conservative way to introduce yourself to a
new wing, and turned out to be the right call.
