No agenda for this one. A casual meetup with pilots to fly the site and enjoy
the fact that Mount Shasta is right there.

## Conditions

A round of overdevelopment had just moved through, which is usually the end of
your day and occasionally the making of it. This was the second kind. What it
left behind was a glass-off — the air smoothing out into the evening — with the
sun going down and enormous clouds still standing near Shasta.

The thermals were not everywhere. There were long stretches of nothing. But the
few I found were strong enough to be serious about: one of them was clearly
willing to take me to 13,000 feet if I stayed with it.

## The flight

That combination is unusual and worth writing down. Sparse, powerful climbs
under a sky that had just finished doing something violent, going quiet, with
Shasta lit up on the side of it.

Not a distance day. Not trying to be. Some flights are just the site being
beautiful and you being lucky enough to be standing on it at the right hour.
