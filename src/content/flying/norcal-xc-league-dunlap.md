Through the summer the Bay Area crowd runs events around California — the NorCal
XC League. They are unofficial, which undersells them: they are competitive, and
the people showing up are the people you want to be flying against.

## The site

The reason this one mattered to me is that it was a new site: Dunlap. A really
fun flying arena, and the kind of place I would go back to on a good forecast
without needing an event as an excuse.

## Conditions

We had some very windy days. That made it challenging, and honestly trying at
points. Windy days at an unfamiliar site are where you find out how much of your
comfort is skill and how much of it is just knowing where everything is.

Still a good weekend, and mostly because of the group. That is the thing about
the league — the scoring is real enough to sharpen you up, and the retrieve is
still eight pilots arguing about the same thermal.
