The day after the XC League weekend, a group of us went out for casual XC at
Tollhouse, which is just up the road from Dunlap.

## Conditions

The forecast said very light wind and a high top of lift. That is the forecast
you want and it is not always the one you get. This time it was exactly right.

Lift everywhere. Not the kind of day where you are hunting and grinding — the
kind where the decision is not *whether* there is a climb ahead of you, but which
one is worth stopping for.

## The flight

Four of us flew 100 km downwind.

There is not much drama to report, which is the point. Some of the best flying
days are the ones where the plan works, nobody has a moment, and the whole group
lands within reach of each other having covered real ground.

## Afterwards

Our driver picked us up, and we got Mexican food on the way back to the
vehicles. That is a full accounting of the day and I would not change a line of
it.
