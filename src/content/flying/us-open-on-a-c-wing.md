Straight on from the Chelan Open into the US Open of Paragliding, at the same
site, with a different field.

## The field

The US Open is where experienced pilots fly open-class CCC and D wings — a
couple of steps above the high C I am on. I stayed on the GTO 3 and had a great
time, which is worth saying plainly: flying a lower-rated wing in that field is
not a handicap you spend the week apologising for. It is just a different
problem to solve, and the days are the same days.

## Conditions

There were quite a few weather days. People who have been coming for years said
more than most past events. That is the tax on a fixed week in one place.

The days that did fly were excellent — the massive, strong thermals Chelan is
known for, and good company in the gaggle. The best FAI triangle I have flown,
98 km, came out of the 21st.

## Afterwards

Two competitions back to back at the same site, a week apart, is a strange and
very effective way to learn a place. By the end of the second one I was making
decisions about the flats that I would have had to think about at the start of
the first.
